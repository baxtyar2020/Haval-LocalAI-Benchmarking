08 · Complete scoring chain

The equations behind every match.

QualityQw = 0.40qprimary + Σ[0.60/(n−1) × qsecondary]
Time normalizationg(r) = 100 if r ≤ 1; otherwise 100 × 2−(r−1)
ExperienceEL=.45gTTFT+.55gTotal · EB=.35gTTFT+.65gTotal · EH=.25gTTFT+.75gTotal
ReliabilityRw = 100 × successful runs / attempted runs
Workload fitWw = Qw.60 × Ew.30 × Rw.10
Persona overallPp = .25WL + .40WB + .35WH
Business fitB = Σ(ωp × Pp), where Σωp = 1
Hardware suitabilityS = 100 × (Q/100).40 × (E/100).35 × (H/100).25
